# TSMC 3Q26: revenue above guidance, growth comes from price and mix, not volume

## Statement
TSMC reported 3Q26 on 2026-10-15 (Tier 1a, IR documents):

- Revenue US$46.72bn, above the guidance range of 44.6 to 45.8; +16.2% QoQ, +41.2% YoY in US$ (NT$1,494.24bn, +17.6% QoQ, +50.9% YoY).
- Gross margin 66.4% (guidance 65 to 67%, 2Q26 67.7%), lower QoQ mainly due to the initial N2 ramp. Operating margin 58.7% (guidance 56 to 58%).
- Net income NT$763.06bn, +8.0% QoQ, +68.7% YoY. QoQ held back by non-operating items falling from NT$95.83bn to 34.59bn (2Q26 contained disposal and mark-to-market gains on VIS shares).
- Wafer shipments 4,476k 12-inch equivalents, +3.2% QoQ, +9.6% YoY.
- Revenue per 12-inch-equivalent wafer (calculated): 1,494.24bn / 4,476k = NT$333.8k; 2Q26 1,270.38 / 4,336 = 293.0k (+13.9%); 3Q25 989.92 / 4,085 = 242.3k (+37.7% YoY).
- Wafer revenue by node: N2 8% (2Q26 3%, 3Q25 0%), N3 33% (30%, 23%), N5 28% (33%, 37%), N7 10%. 7nm and below 79%.
- Revenue by platform: HPC 68% (2Q26 66%, 3Q25 57%), +22% QoQ; smartphone 21% (+10% QoQ); IoT 4%; automotive 4% (+23% QoQ); DCE 1%; others 2%.
- Revenue by geography: North America 79%, Asia Pacific 10%, China 5% (3Q25 8%), Japan 3%, EMEA 3%.
- Inventory days 87 to 82 (TSMC: due to higher wafer shipments). Receivable days 29 to 28.
- Operating cash flow NT$919.77bn, capex NT$443.21bn (US$13.84bn; YTD US$40.64bn), free cash flow NT$476.56bn (2Q26 287.36).
- Capex as share of revenue (calculated, US$): 13.84 / 46.72 = 29.6% (2Q26 15.70 / 40.20 = 39.1%).

## Carries
Reported demand for leading-edge nodes and HPC grew faster than wafer volume in 3Q26: revenue per wafer rose 13.9% QoQ while shipments rose 3.2%. HPC share and the N3+N2 share rose over three consecutive reported quarters (HPC 57 to 66 to 68%; N3+N2 23 to 33 to 41%).

## Does not carry
Earnings of memory makers or any other supplier (see demand-is-not-earnings). The split of HPC into AI accelerators, CPUs and networking: TSMC does not report it. Revenue per wafer mixes price, node mix and wafer size equivalents; the document does not separate them.

## Break indicator
In a later TSMC quarterly report: revenue per wafer flat or down while shipments rise, inventory days rising, or HPC share falling.
