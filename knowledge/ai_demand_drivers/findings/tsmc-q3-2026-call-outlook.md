# TSMC 3Q26 call: management says AI demand exceeds supply, 2026 growth raised to about 44%

## Statement
Guidance and statements from TSMC's 3Q26 earnings call, 2026-10-15:

- 4Q26 guidance: revenue US$52.3 to 54.3bn (midpoint +14% QoQ, calculated 53.3 / 46.72 = 1.141; TSMC: +58% YoY at midpoint), gross margin 65 to 67%, operating margin 56.5 to 58.5%, exchange rate US$1 = NT$31.8.
- Full-year 2026 revenue growth in US$ now around 44%. Earlier guidance during 2026: close to 30% (January), above 30% (April), slightly above 40% (July).
- 2026 capex reiterated at US$60 to 64bn. Capex 2026 to 2028 expected significantly higher than the prior three years.
- Margin dilution from overseas fabs closer to 2% for 2026, widening to 3 to 4% in later years. 4Q gross margin decline attributed to the N2 ramp and overseas fabs.
- C.C. Wei: demand is higher than supply and the gap is still big; customers continue to increase demand; no sign of slowdown. The AI accelerator revenue CAGR given earlier (mid-to-high 50s %, 2024 to 2029) "probably will be higher"; no new number. CPUs for agentic AI are not in that definition.
- Wei: consumer and price-sensitive end markets are challenged by rising prices and macro uncertainty; 2027 planning adjusted accordingly.
- Capacity: N2 family capacity to more than double by 2027. A14 risk production 2027, volume 2028. One additional N3 fab in Tainan (volume 1H28). Arizona fab 2 volume production 3Q27, slightly ahead of schedule. Texas under evaluation; talks with a customer about "Terafab", condition is that TSMC runs the fab.
- Packaging: CoWoS in volume production at 5x reticle size, 9x and 14x in development. Silicon interposers sourced from GlobalFoundries because of back-end shortage.
- Capacity resale between customers: TSMC does not allow subletting or auctioning; mask licensing cases exist, "very, very small".

## Carries
The supplier's own statement, after a quarter above guidance, that leading-edge and advanced-packaging demand exceeds its capacity, and its fourth upward revision of 2026 growth in one year.

## Does not carry
That demand exceeds supply as an independently measured fact: the gap is management's assessment and is not quantified. Announced fabs and capacity targets are plans, not built capacity (see announced-is-not-built). Statements apply to leading-edge nodes and AI; for consumer segments TSMC reports weakness.

## Break indicator
TSMC withdraws or softens the demand-above-supply statement, lowers full-year growth, or cuts capex guidance in a later call.
