# Race on all levels: unverifiable rival position (axiom 3 of 3)

## Statement
Axiom 3 (Mendel, 2026-10-16: "there is a race for everything"; earlier the same day: "there is a race on all levels").

No player in the AI race can verify the rival's position and must therefore assume the unfavourable case. Investing
is dominant, even without proof that a lead compounds. The race runs on all levels: states (USA, China) without
verification and without enforceable agreement, very stable; firms (hyperscalers, labs, neoclouds) with capex
observable each quarter and multi-market contact, less stable; and down the supply chain, where everyone competes for
the same scarce inputs (HBM allocation, CoWoS slots, grid connections, talent). The analogy Middle Ages versus steam
age is a hypothesis, not a reason. Not provable, carried.

Saving money loses weight when winning is at stake (Mendel, 2026-10-16): racers pay for position and speed, not
for the lowest cost per unit. They still buy energy efficiency, because power is the binding constraint, not to
save money. How far this goes depends on who pays (Mendel: "Oracle as a single firm has limited money, whole
countries look different, and then come the huge hyperscalers"):
- States: nominally unlimited through the central bank, the limit is inflation.
- The big four hyperscalers: debt headroom about 1.7 to 2.1 tn USD, money not binding until about 2028 (debt-headroom-big-four-2026).
- Single firms such as Oracle, neoclouds and private labs: financing binds already in 2026 (financing-limit).

The race does not hinge on the weakest buyer: if one runs out of money, its demand moves up the tiers. Weak buyers
get around their limit with customer prepayments (Tencent to Oracle, reported ~30 % upfront, tencent-oracle-lease-2026).

## Carries
The sign: that investment happens, and under which condition it tips. Originally scoped to training compute; widened
by Mendel to all levels, including securing inputs ahead (contracts, prepayments, allocation). Racers' demand for
the newest generation reacts little to price, which gives suppliers of the scarce inputs (memory, packaging)
pricing power.

## Does not carry
Amounts, duration, profitability. Inference volume still follows unit economics (original limitation kept).
Buyers outside the race still count money (older-hbm-hardware-holds-value). For a single firm the money limit
binds; the axiom only says the race as a whole does not stop there. A
prisoner's dilemma exists only if mutual pausing would be better for both; which case applies cannot be read off.

## Break indicator
Primary: the first hyperscaler cuts capex guidance and loses neither market share nor valuation over two quarters.
Lagging: aggregate hyperscaler capex grows below 10 % YoY for two quarters in a row.

## Source
Hyperscaler filings and calls, Tier 1a. Blind for state buyers.
