# Nvidia: beginning or middle of the cycle? (check, Nov 2026)

## Statement
Mendel (2026-11-10): "Bubbles will form, the question is whether we are at the beginning or in the middle."
"Old cycles (2018/19 crypto, 2022, Cisco 2000) say little because the technology changed; there was no LLM in 2000."
"Use 'what if' only with an observed signal."

Snapshot 2026-11-10 (Yahoo Finance, screenshot by Mendel): NVDA 179.82 USD, trailing P/E 23.6, TTM EPS 7.57,
next earnings 17 Nov 2026. Mendel's own forecast table grows data-centre revenue linearly (+~12 bn USD per quarter):
in percent that slows (+69 % q1 26 to q1 27, +18 % q1 30 to q1 31). Mendel: the slowdown is the physical build limit
(CoWoS, HBM, grid, transformers), not demand. At an unchanged price the trailing P/E falls below 20 around the
q4 26 report on that table.

Frame: Perez (2002), four phases: installation, frenzy, turning point (crash), deployment. The crash comes in
the middle; the technology wins afterwards. Bubbles are local (2000: Pets.com to zero, Amazon -90 % and back,
Cisco -80 % for good).

| Signal (as known to mid-2026) | Points to |
|---|---|
| ~10 % of US firms use AI in production (Census BTOS 2025) | early |
| no productivity gain in macro statistics yet (as electricity before 1920, David 1990) | early |
| Nvidia P/E ~23 vs Cisco 2000 ~150 to 200; hyperscaler capex mostly from cash flow | early, healthy |
| old GPUs fully booked, token volume ~10x per year | early |
| circular deals, off-balance-sheet data-centre debt, startup valuations | frenzy (but see circular-financing: size small vs growth) |

Reading Claude 2026-11-10: transition from installation to frenzy; technology and usage early, parts of the
financing frenzy-like. Nvidia sits less in the bubble than private labs and indebted neoclouds, but beta ~2.25.

## Open (what to read, observed signals only)
1. Share of hyperscaler capex financed by debt and off-balance-sheet vehicles vs operating cash flow (see capex-vs-operating-cash-flow-2026).
2. Any big-four guidance "next year capex flat or down".
3. Cloud growth (Azure, Google Cloud, AWS) after the 2024/25 capacity came online: accelerating = capacity earns.
4. H100/A100 rental index and RPO/backlog trend.
5. Share of Nvidia revenue from customers Nvidia holds stakes in.

## Does not carry
A date for the turn (no cycle date). A price target. Not investment advice.
