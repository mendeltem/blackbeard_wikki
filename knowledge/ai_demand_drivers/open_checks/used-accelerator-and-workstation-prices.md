# Used and rented accelerator prices, workstation VRAM prices: primary readings

## Statement
older-hbm-hardware-holds-value rests on trade-press indicators. To read it properly:

1. Nvidia CFO remark on rental prices (+20 % H100, +15 % A100): find the quarter and the exact wording in the call transcript (Q1 FY27, May 2026, or later).
2. SemiAnalysis H100 one-year rental index: original series, latest value after March 2026, and whether A100 and B200 are tracked too.
3. A used-price series for A100 80 GB and H100 80 GB (auction or broker data with dates), not vendor blogs.
4. RTX 5090 street price in Germany as a dated series (e.g. a price-comparison site history) against MSRP.
5. Hyperscaler depreciation: whether any of the big four or a neocloud changed useful life or recorded gains on GPU disposals (10-K/10-Q notes).

## Carries
Turns the mechanism from trade-press indicators into a measured series; item 5 is the only route to Tier 1a.

## Does not carry
Any statement about memory-maker earnings.

## Break indicator
Not applicable, open check. Done when items 1 to 4 have a dated source and item 5 has a filing reference or a "none found".
