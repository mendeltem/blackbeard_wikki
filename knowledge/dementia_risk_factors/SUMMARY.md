2026-10-24, literature check: **neuroticism and Alzheimer's disease.** The association is replicated over two decades
but modest: pooled HR 1.13-1.24, UK Biobank top vs bottom quartile AD HR 1.42, EPIC-Norfolk HR 1.14 per SD. The often
quoted "twice the risk" is a 90th-vs-10th-percentile contrast from small early Rush studies. At autopsy neuroticism is
not related to plaques or tangles; it predicts dementia at equal pathology (lower resilience). PET effects are small
(amyloid r = 0.07, tau r = 0.15) with several null cohorts. The one Mendelian randomization on AD is null; genetic MR
support exists only for white-matter diffusivity, not cortical thickness. The pathway that the data support is vascular
and mental health (depression, anxiety, hypertension; stronger for vascular dementia). See
[neuroticism-and-alzheimer-evidence-check-2026-10](findings/neuroticism-and-alzheimer-evidence-check-2026-10.md).
