# Tencent leases ~100,000 AI chips from Oracle in Southeast Asia (reported, October 2026)

## Statement
Reported by the Financial Times on 2026-10-01, carried by DCD and TrendForce; Reuters could not verify it, Oracle and
Tencent did not comment:
- Five-year lease of about 100,000 AI chips in several Oracle data centres in Southeast Asia, about 7 bn USD.
- About 30 % upfront, roughly 2.1 bn USD, the rest over the term (Dutch IT Channel citing the FT).
- Reason: under US export controls the chips cannot be bought for China; Tencent uses the capacity for model training and agentic tools. Chip type not reported.
- Tencent's free cash flow turned negative in Q2 2026 amid large AI prepayments (as reported).

Calculation: 7 bn USD / 100,000 chips / 5 years = 14,000 USD per chip and year; at full use 14,000 / 8,760 h =
**~1.60 USD per chip-hour**. The H100 one-year rental index stood at 2.35 USD (March 2026, see
older-hbm-hardware-holds-value). So either older or smaller chips, a large volume discount, or 7 bn USD is not the
full contract value.

## Carries
Example for axiom 3 (thesis Mendel, 2026-10-16: "even if no US firm bought compute, there are buyers everywhere"):
export controls reroute Chinese demand through clouds in third countries instead of removing it from the market. For
memory it does not matter who rents: the chips in Oracle's halls carry HBM. Second, the prepayment pattern: a buyer
finances a money-limited intermediary (Oracle, where financing binds already in 2026 per
debt-headroom-big-four-2026), the same pattern as micron-customer-deposits-jump.

## Does not carry
That price does not matter: the implied rate is below the rental index. Any amount as fact: unconfirmed press report.
HBM content: chip type unknown. Not a hyperscaler-sized commitment: 7 bn USD over five years is small against the
big four's annual capex.

## Break indicator
Oracle or Tencent deny the report, or Tencent's next annual report shows no lease or prepayment commitment of this
size.
