# HBM contract prices and volumes for 2027: company readings

## Statement
second-source-hbm4-arrives-presold rests on trade press (HBM up to +50 % in 2027, Samsung asking ~4 USD/Gb for HBM4
against ~1.50 for HBM3E, Micron's 2027 volume contracted). To read it from the companies:

1. SK Hynix Q3 2026 call (27 October US time, 28 October Seoul): is 2027 HBM volume and price agreed, HBM4 share of HBM revenue, direction of HBM price per bit.
2. Samsung Q3 2026 call (late October): 2027 HBM4 terms, share at Nvidia, HBM4 yield comments.
3. Micron FQ1 FY2027 (December 2026): share of 2027 HBM contracted, price direction.
4. Nvidia: whether a server price increase of more than 15 % in early 2027 is confirmed.
5. A dated per-Gb price comparison HBM3E against HBM4 from a company statement, not an analyst.

## Carries
Turns the mechanism's indicators into company statements.

## Does not carry
Any reading before the calls; asking prices are not contract prices.

## Break indicator
Not applicable, open check. Done when the 2027 price direction is confirmed or contradicted by at least two of the
three memory makers.
