Statement

Axiom 2 (Mendel, 2026-10-16: "AI makes everything more productive").

AI raises productivity in every activity it touches, and the technological search space is largely unexplored. Additional compute in search and simulation yields results whose return covers the cost of the compute unit. This holds in both cases: when tools improve faster than search gets harder, and when each discovery needs more compute. Demand is therefore saturated in no application class, across all industries. Not provable, carried.

Carries

The precondition "market not saturated" for the Jevons tool. Breadth of drivers: no single sector has to carry demand (every-sector-grows-sum-counts).

Does not carry

Pace, height, willingness to pay. Not the breadth of buyers: orders run through a few hyperscalers. Measured productivity effects so far are small in places (ai_labor_market: adoption-broad-but-shallow, llm-adoption-null-effect-denmark); they are findings about the current state and stand beside the axiom, they do not test it. The break indicator below does.

Break indicator

Core: return per compute unit falls below its cost. Primary proxy: Jevons falsifier (token growth falls below the efficiency rate). Secondary proxy: demand outside the hyperscalers grows more slowly than hyperscaler demand for two quarters in a row.

Source

Core blind. Primary proxy Tier 1a, irregular. Secondary proxy: Nvidia customer commentary, Tier 1a, inconsistently delimited.
