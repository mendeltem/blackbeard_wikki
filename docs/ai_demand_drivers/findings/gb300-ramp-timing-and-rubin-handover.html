Statement

When B300/GB300 volume rose, read from filings and ODM data, and when Rubin takes over.

Reported (Tier 1a, Nvidia releases and calls; fiscal quarters mapped to calendar months): - Q2 FY26 (May to Jul 2025): GB300 production shipments started; Blackwell Ultra "tens of billions" of revenue; inventory 11 -> 15 bn USD to support the ramp. - Q3 FY26 (Aug to Oct 2025): GB300 crossed GB200, about two thirds of Blackwell revenue. Data center 51.2 bn, networking 8.2 bn. - Q4 FY26 (Nov 2025 to Jan 2026): data center 62.3 bn, networking 11 bn, "Blackwell Ultra ramp". - Q1 FY27 (Feb to Apr 2026): DC compute 60.4 bn, networking 14.8 bn, growth from "Blackwell 300 products". - Q2 FY27 (May to Jul 2026): data center 89.0 bn, driven by Blackwell Ultra; networking +138 % YoY. - Q3 FY27 guide (Aug to Oct 2026): revenue 108 bn, Vera Rubin about 20 % of data center revenue.

Supply chain lag (trade press and broker notes): HBM3E 12-Hi (the B300 stack) shipped from Q4 2024 (SK hynix call, Tier 1a). Quanta GB300 trial production late Q3 2025, mass production Q4 2025; Morgan Stanley (Nov 2025) GB300 rack assembly started, 14 to 15k GB racks in Q4 2025 after 8 to 8.5k in Q3; Quanta: GB300 replaced GB200 as main line in early 2026. Chain: HBM -> Nvidia revenue -> ODM racks, roughly one quarter per step.

Estimate of GB300 revenue (Claude; compute = DC minus networking, Hopper about 2 bn in Q3 FY26; GB300 share of Blackwell after Q3 FY26 is an assumption of 75 to 90 %):

Q2 FY26 ~10 (lower bound of "tens of billions")
Q3 FY26 ~27  (43.0 compute - 2 Hopper = 41 x 2/3)
Q4 FY26 ~40  (51.3 x 75-85 %)
Q1 FY27 ~52  (60.4 x 85-90 %)
Q2 FY27 ~63  (89.0 - ~17.3 networking = 71.7 x 85-90 %)
Q3 FY27 ~51  (guide-derived: ~100 DC - 20 Rubin - ~20 networking, then 85 %)

At a 65 % GB300 share the peak would be about 47 instead of 63 bn; the shape stays.

Rubin handover: Nvidia guides first Vera Rubin shipments Q3 2026, volume ramp Q4 2026, growth through H1 2027. Jefferies: Rubin about 12 % of GPU revenue in Q3 FY27, above 40 % in Q4 FY27, possibly the largest contributor in Q1 FY28. Rough: DC ~115 bn in Q4 FY27 (assumption +15 % QoQ), compute ~93, >40 % -> above 37 bn Rubin. Racks in data centres one to two quarters later, Feb to Apr 2027, possibly shorter (Rubin compute tray assembly about 5 instead of about 120 minutes, per analysts). Probability (Claude) that Rubin is at least 35 % of compute revenue in Q4 FY27: about 70 %.

TrendForce (April 2026): Blackwell over 70 % of Nvidia high-end GPU shipments in 2026, led by GB300/B300; Rubin cut from 29 to 22 % because of HBM4 qualification and other delays.

Carries

Timing: Nvidia's GB300 revenue inflected in Aug to Oct 2025, ODM rack volume in Q4 2025 to Q1 2026; GB300 revenue peaked around May to Jul 2026; Rubin's revenue ramp is Nov 2026 to Jan 2027.

Does not carry

GB300 dollar figures after Q3 FY26: Nvidia does not split them, these are estimates. Not unit counts. Rack timing rests on one cycle of lag.

Break indicator

  • Nvidia's Q3 FY27 report (around 2026-11-19) guides Rubin clearly below about 35 % of compute for Q4 FY27.
  • ODM monthly revenue (Foxconn, Quanta, Wistron, around the 10th of each month) shows GB300 rack volume rising into 2027: GB300 plateau longer than assumed.
  • Further HBM4 or thermal-lid delays (KeyBanc July 2026, KYEC guidance cut) push Rubin volume into 2027.
