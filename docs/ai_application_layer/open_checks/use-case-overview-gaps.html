Aussage

The 2026-10-05 list was a keyword guess against a 98-card page. On 2026-10-17 the live page (mendeltem.github.io/ai_use_cases_overview) was read directly: the array const UC holds 144 cards in 13 labelled cluster blocks (Mobility, warehouse & energy, industry & humanoids, agriculture, medicine, hazardous, science, AI on the local workstation, security & fraud, back office, city, enablers, compute & energy substrate) — the cluster numbering has gaps, so the "18 clusters" of the SUMMARY come from more than this one array. Every candidate was then matched against card titles plus card text, and the confirmed gaps were checked against primary sources.

Confirmed gaps — no card. Five with a primary source, two without one (marked). - Cashierless checkout (Just Walk Out). No card. Amazon's FY2025 10-K names "Amazon's Just Walk Out technology" only inside a biometric-privacy class action; no deployment or store figure. So the technology is named in a filing but production scale is unverified. - ANPR and road tolling. No card. Verra Mobility FY2025 10-K (CIK 1682745, filed 2026-02-24): Commercial Services $435.8M revenue 2025 (≈45 % of total), toll management ≈39 % and violation management ≈4 % of 2025 revenues, Parking Solutions $82.6M (≈8 %) with ≈1,775 parking customers, violation collection for foreign-registered vehicles in 20 European countries; "automated license plate recognition" is named. Shows: ANPR is the operating core of a listed photo-enforcement company with real revenue, not a pilot. This is the strongest new card candidate. - AI credit scoring / underwriting. No card (only Fraud/AML banking, Insurance claims, Robo-advisory). Upstart FY2025 10-K (CIK 1647639): total revenue $1,043.9M in 2025 (2024: $636.5M), revenue from fees net $950.0M (+49 %), net income $53.6M, and the platform's approval decisions run on "our AI models"; in 2025 the top three lending partners originated 83 % of the loans facilitated through the marketplace. Shows: AI underwriting at a listed lender with real volume, and the concentration risk that comes with it. - Hearing aids with AI. No card. Demant Annual Report 2025 states verbatim that "the most advanced hearing aids feature deep neural networks (DNNs) and use artificial intelligence (AI) to understand the sound scene"; the same report puts long-run market growth at ~6 % per year in units (upper end of a 4–6 % structural range) and estimates that ~13 million people were fitted with ~23 million hearing aids worldwide in 2025. Sonova FY2025/26: group sales CHF 3,605.9M (+5.9 % local), Hearing Instruments segment CHF 3,353.8M (+7.5 % local), normalized EBITA CHF 811.2M; the release credits the "Infinio platform, including Infinio Ultra" with "AI-enabled hearing aid performance". Neither gives AI-attributable unit numbers. Shows: DNN-based hearing aids are a described, shipped product class in the two largest vendors' own reports. - Content moderation. No card. TaskUs FY2025 10-K (CIK 1829864): Trust & Safety is a named reporting service line that "consists of two primary areas of service: Content Moderation and Financial Crime & Compliance"; the company supported ~200 clients as of 2025-12-31 and also sells AI Services (LLM support, data labelling) — but the filing names no AI system doing the moderation itself. Shows: content moderation is a real, reported business line, while the AI-in-the-loop claim is still unproven from this source. - Air traffic control. No card; no source found this session. Unverified. - Mail sorting with handwriting recognition. No card; USPS OIG search endpoints returned 406/403 from this network, no independent source found. Unverified.

Not gaps — the 2026-10-05 keyword pass was wrong here. - Accessibility: cards "Be My Eyes" and "Be My AI" exist. - Rail track inspection: "Rail predictive maintenance" (DB DIANA, Weichen-EKG) exists; the page's own note is right. - Port automation: "AI container terminals" exists. The suspected "port = support" false positive does not apply. - Delivery dispatch: "Logistics orchestration" (Walmart, DHL, Locus), "Sidewalk delivery robots" (Starship, Serve), "Autonomous trucks" and "Delivery & medical drones" already cover road and last-mile dispatch; a separate card is not obviously warranted. - Insurance underwriting, steel/cement process control, pest control, factory predictive maintenance, payment fraud, telecom, mining, seismic, legal, HR, customer service, drug discovery, weather, translation, games, vision QC all have cards. Of the 11 Wednesday candidates only the seven listed above survived; the rest were already covered.

New candidates found by holding industries without a card against known production cases. Automotive OEM manufacturing (the word "automotive" appears only in the Catena-X data-space card), consumer ADAS (Super Cruise / BlueCruise, distinct from the robotaxi card), personalised advertising (probably the single largest AI revenue line and absent), voice assistants, writing/coding assistants, e-commerce search and recommendation, fast-food drive-thru AI, grocery demand forecasting, hotel revenue management. None of these was verified against a primary source this session — they are candidates, not findings.

Trägt nicht

That "no card" means no deployment — it means the page has no card. Some candidates (ATC, mail sorting) may still turn out to be documented elsewhere. The card stock also moves: 98 → 143 → 144 cards within twelve days, so every count here is a snapshot of 2026-10-17.

Bruchindikator

A candidate confirmed as a real production case and still without a card after the next pass.
