Camera-only gaze on a phone: about 2-3 cm with landmark features and a per-person regression

TypBefundStatusvorschlagStand2026-10-21HerkunftClaude (Alita), literature check 2026-10-21 and own app mendeltem/camera-eye-tracker (fce963a)Zuletzt geändert2026-10-21 webcam_eye_tracking: camera-only gaze on phones, sources checked, live appVerwiesen vonMoving head or phone after calibration: fix the features and calibrate with movement, not the gyroscope (webcam_eye_tracking)Quelleknowledge/webcam_eye_tracking/findings/camera-only-gaze-on-phones.md

Aussage

Gaze from the normal front camera, in the browser, lands around 2-3 cm of error on a phone after a short calibration (own estimate from the studies below, not measured on a real phone yet). Trained CNNs with heavy personal calibration reach 0.5 cm, but only with large training sets and outlier users removed. The method that runs as a static page: MediaPipe Face Landmarker (iris points), iris offset measured in each eye's own frame, head pose and face position as extra features, ridge regression with lambda chosen by leave-one-dot-out cross-validation, One Euro smoothing, blink frames skipped, held-out test dots scored before they join the training set.

Trägt

A browser demo or study that only needs which third of a phone screen someone looks at (three large targets, dwell selection), no installation, nothing uploaded. Camera position is free: features in the eye's own frame plus head pose let the calibration learn where the camera sits.

Trägt nicht

Reading single words, small targets, long sessions without recalibration (drift), glasses with reflections, dark rooms, a phone turned after calibration. WebGazer code is GPL-3.0 and GazeCapture data is research-only: do not copy either into an MIT repo.

Bruchindikator

The app's own test-dot error on a real phone well above 20 % of the screen diagonal in good light, or a study showing landmark-plus-ridge on phones far worse than 3 cm.

Ablesung

Live: https://mendeltem.github.io/camera-eye-tracker/ (2026-10-21). Headless Chromium with a fake camera passes the whole chain (10/10 steps, also against the live site); the test cannot measure accuracy because a still photo does not follow the dots. Real-phone error: not measured yet.
