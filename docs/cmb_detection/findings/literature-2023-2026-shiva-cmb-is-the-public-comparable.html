Aussage

A literature review of automatic CMB detection on SWI/T2* (PubMed + Europe PMC, 37 retrieved records; arXiv not reachable, HTTP 429 through the proxy) gives three actionable results for this project:

  1. The closest published analogue of our chain is SHIVA-CMB. One 3D U-Net trained once on 450 scans from 7 acquisitions and 6 cohorts, T2 and SWI mixed (Tsuchida 2024, PMID 39730628): held-out test sensitivity 0.67, precision 0.82, F1 (Dice) 0.74, 0.6 false positives per scan; on an evaluation-only set with acquisitions never seen in training 0.67 / 0.91 / 0.77 / 0.5 FP; correlation with expert visual counts in 1,992 T2-weighted scans Pearson R 0.89. Both the pipeline (SHiVAi) and the pretrained weights (SHIVA-CMB) are public -- a free external baseline we should run on our data.
  2. Precision, not sensitivity, separates the methods. The best lesion-level numbers reported 2023-2026 come from pipelines whose candidate stage is deliberately constrained: morphology filter bank + CNN, 95.0 % sensitivity at 4.37 FP/patient versus 97.5 % at 25.87 FP without the filter bank (Nishioka 2025, PMID 38494702); a geometric-to-neural cascade (GMM candidate generation + two 3D ResNets on QA labels) removing 76.8 % of stage-A false positives, F1 0.693, AUC 0.9587 (Bogdanov 2026 preprint, DOI 10.64898/2026.07.24.740624); U-Net with CBAM attention, large kernels and anatomy localisation, F1 0.887 at 0.83 FP/scan on 506 multi-site scans (Cho 2026, PMID 41859233). This matches our own negative finding that sensitivity levers do not raise lesion F1.
  3. Mimic handling is a contrast problem, not an architecture problem. QSM separates CMB from calcification better than SWI/phase on accuracy, confidence and inter-rater agreement (Kang 2025, PMID 39690177); adding a high-pass filtered phase channel to SWI gave 95.8 % sensitivity, 70.9 % precision, 1.6 FP/case (Liu 2019, PMID 31121296); multi-modal T2w/SWI/QSM input improved CMB-versus-iron separation, QSM being the most informative channel (Rashid 2021, PMID 34238951). Anatomical masking (ventricles/CSF/choroid plexus/cerebellum/edges) is a free false-positive filter present in the top pipelines -- consistent with our CSF rule.

Secondary results: synthetic CMBs are now a first-class training strategy (analytic model, 37,000 lesions released, ~9 FP/scan when trained on synthetic only, Momeni 2021, PMID 34051412; 3D lesion GAN transferring to unseen MRI parameters, PMID 34975381; self-supervised pseudo-labels giving +46 % average precision on ex-vivo T2, Nikseresht 2026, PMID 42107619). The SWI/T2 domain gap is measurable: synthesising SWI from T2 magnitude recovers 19/28 CMBs seen on real SWI versus 10/28 on magnitude images (Genc 2023, PMID 36733222). Meta-analytic pooled DL accuracy is high (lesion-level sensitivity 0.96 / specificity 0.98, patient-level 0.89 / 0.86, Feng 2026, PMID 42767630), but the authors themselves warn the study count is small. SWI detects about 1.6x more CMBs than T2-GRE (13-study meta-analysis, Yang 2026, PMID 41775374), so sequence mix dominates any single F1 number.

Trägt

SHIVA-CMB publishes pipeline and weights, so it is a reproducible external baseline (claim 1). Claims 2 and 3 rest on peer-reviewed abstracts retrieved in this task and are consistent with our own measured results (vessel inpainting, CSF rule, sensitivity levers).

Trägt nicht

  • No CMB-specific transformer or foundation model was retrieved. The only retrieved foundation-model work is a universal brain-MRI lesion segmentation model with Mixture-of-Modality-Experts (9 datasets, 8 lesion types, CMB not among them, Zhang 2025, PMID 40031588); the foundation-model and diffusion reviews report no CMB numbers.
  • No post-2021 successor benchmark to VALDO was retrieved, so the VALDO weak-label and case-level-inconsistency findings stand unresolved.
  • arXiv was not retrievable in this task (HTTP 429 / "Rate exceeded." through the proxy across many retries), so computer-science preprints are entirely missing from this picture; treat the transformer/foundation-model blank as "not retrieved", not as "does not exist".

Bruchindikator

A peer-reviewed work reporting CMB detection with a transformer or foundation model, or a new multi-centre CMB benchmark superseding VALDO, or a paper showing a per-dataset-trained 3D U-Net beating a single multi-cohort model (SHIVA-CMB) on unseen acquisitions.
