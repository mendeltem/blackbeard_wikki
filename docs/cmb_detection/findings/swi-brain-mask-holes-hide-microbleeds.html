A brain mask cut at "image > 0" punches holes exactly where SWI phase masking drove microbleeds to zero

Aussage

SWI magnitude images multiplied with a phase mask can contain voxels of exactly 0 inside the brain, at the darkest spots: microbleeds and haemorrhage remnants. A preprocessing step that intersects the brain mask with "image > 0" removes these voxels; after masking and contrast inversion they are black instead of bright, so the network cannot see the lesion. In-house SWI: about 8 % of the reference microbleeds touched such a hole, and every scan had holes; raw SWI was exactly 0 in about three quarters of the hole voxels. T2-GRE of the same site: 0.5 %; public VALDO T2: 0. Fix: fill the brain mask (3D fill-holes plus slice-wise axial fill), rebuild the inputs from the raw scan inside the filled mask (a raw 0 inside the brain becomes the brightest value after inversion). After the fix 0.8 % of the reference lesions remained outside the mask, all at the true brain edge, none in a hole.

Trägt

Overlay QC page (image, mask, holes, reference lesions) on all scans of three cohorts, before and after; unit test (enclosed hole filled, open notch kept).

Trägt nicht

Absolute SWI detection numbers measured before the fix are too low by an unknown amount; comparisons between methods on the same inputs stay fair.

Bruchindikator

A SWI pipeline without phase masking (raw magnitude only), or a skull strip that does not use an intensity cut.

Ablesung

Own QC 2026-10-30 (aggregates only). SWI phase masking: Haacke et al., Magn Reson Med 2004;52:612-618, doi:10.1002/mrm.20198, PMID 15334582.
