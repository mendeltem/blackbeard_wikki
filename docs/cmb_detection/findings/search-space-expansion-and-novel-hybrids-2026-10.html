Aussage

1. The headroom sits in stage 2, and stage 2 is where new models can be measured. With a perfect candidate classifier our chain would reach about F1 0.85 on VALDO instead of about 0.65 (candidates reached at stage-1 threshold 0.15). The seed spread of stage 2 on fixed candidates is about 10x smaller than that of a stage-1 training (VALDO sd 0.009 against about 0.08 between single stage-1 seeds; in-house cohorts smaller still), and one stage-2 arm costs about one GPU hour. New models therefore go into stage 2 first; stage-1 changes need about 6 GPU hours per comparison and can only resolve effects of about 0.08.

2. Backbone swaps are exhausted, by our data and by the literature. 15 stage-1 networks lost to the anisotropic U-Net in our arena. CenSynCMB's own re-implementations on VALDO Task 2 give SwinUNETR F1 54.3 and VISTA3D 56.4 against 65.8 for an Attention U-Net backbone [1]; nnU-Net Revisited finds Mamba layers "have no effect" [14]; SAM-Med3D removed all training masks below 1 cm3 [15], so CMB-sized objects lie outside it. No CMB-specific transformer, Mamba or foundation detector was found.

3. Most published levers are smaller than our stage-1 noise. CenSynCMB's physics synthesis moves VALDO F1 71.7 -> 73.1 (+CMB) -> 74.3 (+mimics), FP/subject 1.4 -> 0.8, single seed, CI about +-9, no paired synthesis test p<0.05 [1]. Effects of +1 to +4 F1 cannot be seen with two stage-1 seeds; another reason to test in stage 2 or on the CPU.

4. Untried options with the best evidence (audit of 31 CMB sources against our inventory): - Heterogeneous ensembles across architectures: VALDO EnsembleTop (mean of the top 4 teams) median F1 per subject 75.68 [38.18; 100] against 68.42 for the best single team [2]. We ensembled seeds, grids and recipe variants (blob loss) of one architecture, never different architectures. Lower prior here than in VALDO: our runner-up networks are 0.06 or more below the winner, while the VALDO top teams were close to each other. A CPU test on existing maps settles it. - Hard Sample Prototype Learning (feature of the hardest negative voxel per crop pushed away from a CMB prototype): FP/subject 14.73 -> 0.86 at 94.66 % sensitivity together with a feature-fusion module, on SWI+phase [3]. - Synthetic vessel mimics as an explicit label class: annotation-free SynthSeg-style training reaches CMB AUPRC 0.538 against 0.004 for a Frangi baseline on 13 T2* cases [4]; synthetic-only training keeps about 87 % of real-trained sensitivity, but transfer depends on the framework [5]. - Expert-audited labels: VALDO labels are "weak and noisy" [2], nnU-Net false positives on VALDO "could be an actual microbleed" [24]; SHIVA-CMB built its labels in an expert loop at p>=0.05 [19]; missing-annotation mining gave +2.3 sensitivity / +2.7 precision on DeepLesion [25]. - Already measured by us and therefore NOT open (correction 2026-10-25, after a first draft listed it as untried): T1+T2 as extra channels on VALDO (2026-09-29, two seeds, CSF rule): ensemble 0.628 against 0.647 for the base ensemble, no gain; no published ablation exists either [1,2]. A real nnU-Net v2 run on our folds was trained (2026-10-02); its evaluation is still open. - An independent second candidate source (FRST peaks, per-scan GMM [21]) united with the U-Net candidates, to lift the ceiling of stage 2.

5. Adjacent small-object fields: what transfers. Our stage-1 logic (segmentation, connected components, max score) is the "DetU-Net" baseline of Retina U-Net: 3D LIDC mAP10 36.6 against 49.8, lost mainly through high-confidence false positives [6]; nnDetection made the Retina U-Net template safe for small objects [7]. A jointly trained, decoupled false-positive branch raised nodule detection by 10.27 % [8]. Top-K centre-point matching beats a single-centroid CenterNet by 21 points sensitivity at 1 FP/scan [9], a plausible reason why our centre-heatmap head failed. 3D group convolutions reach the FROC of a CNN trained on ten times more data (0.550 -> 0.767 at 300 training samples) [10]; our pooled classifier has about a hundred positive VALDO candidates. Counter-evidence: detection-head re-implementations score 33.6-61.0 on VALDO against 65.8 [1], so a detector is a test, not a promise.

7. Proposed new mixed model, stage 2: "TrackSet-CMB". Built on the fixed out-of-fold candidates of the anisotropic U-Net; every module is one arena switch, so each can be ablated: - (a) local crop encoder (image, FRST, probability, 16 mm) with in-plane D4 equivariance plus z-flip (full 3D groups are invalid on 5 mm slices) [10,32]; - (b) slice-track encoder: per slice above and below the candidate, a 2D crop re-centred on the local intensity minimum (so a curved vessel stays centred), shared 2D encoder, small transformer over the slice tokens with their offsets and contrast (novel by the search; a learned form of the track-length rule that helped on one external SWI set only); - (c) soft context: SynthSeg region, distance to brain edge and ventricles, replacing the hard CSF rule (which deletes real deep CMB on SWI); - (d) one attention layer across all candidates of the scan with a geometric bias (distance, collinearity) [11]; - losses: BCE with positive weight plus the prototype margin loss of [3]; optional third class "vessel mimic" trained with counterfactual renderings (same host, same renderer, sphere vs B0-oriented cylinder). As a whole this combination was not found; (b) and the orientation-aware counterfactual mimics are the novel parts.

8. Proposed stage-1 hybrid (port, not novel): anisotropic U-Net trunk with three heads: segmentation (as now), top-K centre-point matching [9] and a decoupled early-feature false-positive head [8], trained jointly with the prototype loss [3]; objects are scored by the heads instead of connected components plus threshold [6].

9. Measurement order with rules fixed in advance. - Phase 0, CPU only: heterogeneous ensemble from existing out-of-fold maps; ceiling of the candidate union (FRST/GMM + U-Net, about 4 CPU minutes); neighbour-candidate features (k nearest candidates) into the existing pooled classifier as a cheap proxy for (d); the blinded expert review (waiting since 2026-09-22). - Phase 1, stage 2 only, 3 seeds per arm, about 1 GPU hour each: (a), (a)+prototype loss, (b), (d), rendered mimics, inpainting residual. An arm stays if its paired gain exceeds twice the stage-2 seed spread, the case bootstrap interval excludes 0 on the pooled F1, and no cohort gets significantly worse; then confirm the combination with an independent stage-1 seed. Renderer first passes a realism probe (a classifier must not tell rendered from real crops) before any training, as our augmentation lesson requires. - Phase 2, stage 1, 2 seeds, ceiling check before each run: evaluate the trained nnU-Net v2 (calibration), nnDetection, the stage-1 hybrid. - Predictions written before measuring: (a) helps most where positives are fewest; (b) helps on thin slices and is neutral on 5 mm slices, where true CMB also span several slices; rendered mimics lower vessel false positives only if they pass the realism probe.

Not recommended (evidence against): Mamba backbones [14], SAM-Med3D / VISTA3D / BrainSegFounder for tiny lesions [15,1], DETR-type detectors with a few hundred lesions, through-plane super-resolution as preprocessing [17], more seed ensembles.

Nachtrag 2026-10-25: gebaut und eingereiht (noch nichts gemessen)

  • TrackSet-CMB is code now (second stage of our arena): modules d4, track, ctx, set, proto as switches, plus three control arms that the equivariance literature asks for: every arm trains with random in-plane D4 + z-flip augmentation, tta8 averages over the 8 in-plane views at test time, wide gives the plain CNN about the compute of the D4 encoder. Reason: the G-CNN advantage shrinks with data (FROC +0.217 at 300 training samples, +0.059 at 3,000 [10]) and augmentation plus test-time averaging can match equivariance [36]; the d4 arm counts only if it beats both controls. Checked on synthetic phantoms (CPU): the D4 group convolution is equivariant to float64 precision and the network invariant (the plain control is not); the slice track follows an oblique tube and loses a ball; offsets rotate with the patch; test-time averaging makes the score rotation-invariant; the old classifier modes give byte-identical results after the change.
  • Pre-registered decisions in code: one seed screens (advance if delta >= +0.01 in one cohort and no cohort <= -0.02); three seeds decide (mean delta on all cohorts > 2 x the control's seed sd, interval above 0, no cohort entirely below 0).
  • Independent candidate source (Phase 0): FRST peaks or a per-scan Gaussian-mixture threshold [21], united with the U-Net map at a low probability, so U-Net candidates stay unchanged and new ones enter visibly weak.
  • Physics renderer for microbleed, calcification and vein on gradient-echo magnitude: sphere field (chi/3)(a/r)^3(3cos^2 t - 1) with a field-free core [38]; cylinder inside (chi/6)(3cos^2 theta - 1), outside (chi/2)(R/rho)^2 sin^2 theta cos 2phi [37]; venous chi = Hct[(1 - Y) 3.39 - 0.38] ppm SI [39]; magnitude of the voxel-averaged complex signal over the acquisition voxel including slice thickness (static dephasing [40]). Checks: blooming ratio TE 40/20 ms = 1.60 for a 2 mm, 1 ppm sphere at 3 T (measured in patients 1.49-2.30 [41]); a vein perpendicular to B0 dephases about three times the volume of a parallel one; calcium and blood of equal |chi| give the same magnitude image. Not yet built: inserting rendered objects into whole host volumes and recomputing FRST and the stage-1 probability, without which a rendered crop cannot stand next to real candidates.
  • Two orders for the local agent: CPU Phase 0, and the ten stage-2 arms through the GPU queue (behind any DEMDAS work).

Trägt

Every ID was re-fetched by script on 2026-10-25 (arXiv API, Crossref; PMIDs via PubMed E-utilities) and the title matched. The numbers in points 2-5 were re-read in the abstracts or PDF tables (VALDO Table 8, CenSynCMB Tables I and IV, G-CNN Table 2, NoduleNet and Cao abstracts). The tried inventory comes from our own measurements on VALDO and the in-house cohorts (only relative statements here).

Trägt nicht

Agents read many sources at abstract level; HSPL [3] was read in full by one agent but not re-checked here. All adjacent-field gains are on CT nodules or aneurysms, not on CMB. The novelty verdicts rest on about 30 targeted searches (PubMed, arXiv, web) and are no proof of absence. The CenSynCMB external AIBL set is very likely the same public SWI collection as our external set, under a different (per-subject macro) metric, so its 65.0 is not comparable with our pooled F1. TrackSet-CMB and the stage-1 hybrid are designs, not results.

Bruchindikator

A published CMB paper with a learned slice-track or orientation-aware mimic renderer; a Phase-0 ceiling or ensemble result that shows no headroom; stage-2 arms that cannot beat twice the seed spread.
