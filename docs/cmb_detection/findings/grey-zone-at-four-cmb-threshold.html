At the four-CMB treatment threshold, a radiologist grey zone moves decisions -- a better classifier does not

When a public detector over-detects at a new site, test "re-choose the threshold on the n local scans" before claiming a fine-tuning gain (cmb_detection)Quelleknowledge/cmb_detection/findings/grey-zone-at-four-cmb-threshold.md

Aussage

For the anti-amyloid exclusion rule (more than four CMB; Cummings et al. 2023), the adapted two-stage detector at two clinical sites flagged every patient truly above the threshold, but placed a sizeable share of eligible patients above it too. Four ways to raise specificity were compared on the same out-of-fold candidates: - a patient-level threshold chosen inside the cross-validation, a seed ensemble, and three newer stage-2 classifiers (test-time augmentation, prototype head, slice tracking): none moved patient-level specificity beyond its bootstrap interval -- although slice tracking raised SWI lesion F1 by about +0.08 (interval excluding 0); - a grey zone: patients with a detector count of 3-8 go to a radiologist. Specificity of the remaining automatic decisions rose by roughly +0.2 to +0.3 (absolute) while no patient above the threshold was missed, with a minority of the patients referred. Fixed in advance; neighbouring bands (2-6, 4-10, 2-10) gave the same picture, and a band chosen inside the cross-validation (narrowest band reaching sensitivity and specificity >= 0.95 on the other folds; 4-8 in four folds, 4-6 in one) referred fewer patients and kept most of the gain.

Why: errors of a count-based rule cluster near the cut-off; far from it the detector is almost always on the right side, so better lesion detection changes few decisions.

Traegt

Clinical use of CMB detectors for count thresholds: use the detector to triage (decide the clear cases, refer the band around the cut-off) instead of tuning lesion F1 further; report patient-level endpoints next to lesion F1.

Traegt nicht

A treatment-screening population: the cohorts were not selected as anti-amyloid candidates, so burden and the share in the band will differ. "Presence of any CMB" is a different question (one false positive flips it) and stays weak.

Bruchindikator

On a screening population or an external site, the band refers the majority of the patients, or a classifier change moves specificity beyond its interval.
