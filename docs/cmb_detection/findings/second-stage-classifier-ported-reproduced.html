Aussage

The small 3D CNN that re-checks every first-stage candidate (components above 0.15, 16 x 32 x 32 crop of image, FRST and probability, 3 members) was ported from the research chain into the public training package. Trained with a nested 5-fold CV on the out-of-fold maps of a T2* run, it raised pooled lesion F1 by +0.028 over the first stage alone (score threshold chosen out of fold), in line with the earlier research-chain result on the same kind of data.

Trägt

Same candidate rule, crop, members and schedule as the research chain; nested CV (fold k scored by a classifier that never saw fold k).

Trägt nicht

One run, one seed of the first stage; the seed spread of the second stage was 0.003-0.009 in ten-seed measurements of the research chain, the first stage spreads more. In-house numbers stay private (relative only).

Bruchindikator

A second seed of the first stage where the classifier does not improve on the first stage.

Ablesung

Two-stage design after Sundaresan et al., Front Neuroinform 2023;17:1204186, doi:10.3389/fninf.2023.1204186. Package: https://github.com/mendeltem/cmb_3d_unet_experiments (cmbx stage2). Own run 2026-10-16.
