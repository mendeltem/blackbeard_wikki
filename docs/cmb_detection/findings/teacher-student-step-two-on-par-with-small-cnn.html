With a trained teacher, MicrobleedNet's teacher-student step 2 is on par with a small CNN

TypBefundStatusvorschlagStand2026-10-16Herkunftcmb_3d_unet_experiments (cmbx stage2 --method teacher_student vs cnn, nested 5-fold CV on the out-of-fold candidates of one T2* run of an in-house cohort), 2026-10-16Zuletzt geändert2026-10-16 Lessons 16.10.: same-session sequences align by header (voxel-space FLIRT rotated LPS vs RAS), teacher-student step 2 with trained teacher = small CNN, data-set handover without copies, every build installed from zeroVerweist aufMicrobleedNet's published code never trains the teacher of its second stage (cmb_detection)

Aussage

MicrobleedNet's candidate discrimination (teacher = feature extractor + voxel-wise segmentor + patch classifier, student distilled with CE + 0.4 T^2 KL + 0.6 CE, T = 4), rebuilt for a 16 x 32 x 32 crop and with the teacher actually trained, raised pooled lesion F1 over the first stage by +0.030; the small 3D CNN on the same candidates by +0.028. The two are equal within the seed spread of the second stage (0.003-0.009). The failure of the original's step 2 on our data (student at chance) is therefore a property of its code path (teacher never trained), not of the teacher-student idea.

Traegt

Same 1126 candidates, same folds, nested CV for both.

Traegt nicht

One first-stage run, one seed per method, one cohort; absolute numbers of the in-house cohort stay private.

Bruchindikator

A ten-seed comparison in which one of the two is consistently ahead by more than 0.01.

Ablesung

Sundaresan V et al. Front Neuroinform 2023;17:1204186, doi:10.3389/fninf.2023.1204186; Hinton G, Vinyals O, Dean J, arXiv:1503.02531 (2015). Package https://github.com/mendeltem/cmb_3d_unet_experiments (cmbx stage2 --method teacher_student). Own run 2026-10-16.
