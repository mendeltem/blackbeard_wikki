Naming (user, 2026-10-25): Arena 1 = the U-Net tournaments and the earlier stage-2 tests; Arena 2 = the new hybrid models (TrackSet-CMB stage 2, independent candidate source, physics renderer) and everything measured from now on.

Aussage

1. Decision rules (R2): the first rules would have let a null arm through about every fifth time -- fixed before any run. A simulation of 10 null arms against one shared control gave a family-wise false-KEEP rate of 17-23 % (agents' simulation): one bootstrap per arm, seed variance missing from the interval, a 2-df threshold, no multiplicity. Rules v2 (in code): one bootstrap per draw shared by all arms and stratified by cohort, max-T (Dunnett-type) simultaneous lower bound at a family-wise 10 %, seed variance in the interval (pooled, floor 0.009), margin +0.01 on all cohorts, shrunk deltas against the winner's curse, and the word 'carry to confirmation' instead of 'keep': the survivor is tested once on cases never used (the external public SWI set). On 40 simulated all-null runs v2 carried no arm; a true +0.02 arm is confirmed only rarely with ~190 scans -- this arena can select, not confirm small gains.

2. Hyperparameter search (R1). Only one Bayesian search ever ran (Arena 1, one fold, short runs). Stage 1: not worth it -- with a seed sd of 0.08 the best of 30 trials looks about +0.16 better from noise alone (agents' calculation). Stage 2: worth it after the arm decision: ~40 TPE trials, one seed each, pruning on folds not epochs, top 5 + default re-run with three new seeds and new fold assignment, final estimate on an untouched outer fold; tuning and reporting on the same CV is optimistic [1].

3. Labels are a lever of unknown size (R6). Single readers find 39-88 % of a four-reader CMB consensus [2]; with a reference that holds a share s of the true lesions a perfect detector scores at most F1 = 2s / (1 + s) (no false labels), i.e. 0.58-0.94 for s = 0.41-0.88 -- our 'perfect stage 2' ceiling of 0.85 lies inside that band. How many audited 'false positives' are real ranged from 2.6 % [2] to 54 % (LUNA16 pooled FPs) and must be measured: about 650 blinded reads in six strata, two readers plus an adjudicator, symmetric re-scoring of all arms with inclusion-probability weights. Cheap stage-2 use: relabel-and-ignore, nnPU loss with the audited prior.

4. Synthetic insertion (R3). Precedent: FDA nodule insertion into whole CT exams, readers told inserted from real nodules with AUC 0.52-0.71 [3]. Insertion helps most with little data (CarveMix +4.5 Dice at 100 %, +12.9 at 12.5 % of the data [4]); no study compares patch- with volume-level insertion, so run volume-level with stage 1 frozen plus a crop-only ablation. Realism gate: classifier two-sample test on candidate crops WITH a known-bad positive control, not FID. Do not insert calcifications as negatives: on magnitude they equal a microbleed of the same moment; vein mimics are the negative class.

5. Data (R4). No new labelled CMB data appeared 2025-2026. Most useful new resource: QTAB (OpenNeuro ds004146, CC0) with magnitude, phase, SWI and minIP per session at 3 T [5,6] -- for a T2* -> pseudo-SWI model and insertion into real complex data. UK Biobank swMRI (0.8 x 0.8 x 3 mm, magnitude + phase, tens of thousands of participants) needs an application [7].

6. Vein priors (R5). Venous atlases in MNI space exist (Bernier 2018 from 3 T SWI [8], Ward 2018 [9], Huck 2019 from 7 T QSM [10]); no public vein segmenter for SWI/T2* magnitude was found. Cheapest test: 3-5 atlas and SynthSeg-geometry values at the candidate as stage-2 features after a contrast-agnostic registration (SynthMorph [11]); first a 30-minute AUC check; learned feature only, never a hard rule.

7. Clinical targets (R7). The only regulator-reviewed tool with public numbers: icobrain aria (510(k) K240712, 2D FLAIR + 2D T2-GRE), lesion TPR 66.1 % at 0.9 FP per case [12]. Decisions turn at 0/1, 1/2, 4/5 and 9/10 microhaemorrhages (anti-amyloid eligibility and ARIA-H severity), Boston criteria 2.0 count lobar lesions [13]. Report FROC at 0.5/1/2 FP per scan, category agreement per patient and count error by count bin, T2 and SWI separately.

8. Fusion (R8). LUNA16 is our chain one step further: the union of five candidate generators raised candidate sensitivity 92.9 -> 98.3 % and averaging five FP-reduction networks on the SHARED candidate list raised CPM 0.908 -> 0.952 [14]. Our union of seeds raised the ceiling but not F1 because stage 2 was not trained on the union and only one scorer was used. Recipe: union list, the vote count (how many stage-1 runs found the object) as a feature, several stage-2 arms averaged in logit space, one temperature on out-of-fold scores; members must be independent pipelines, not seeds.

9. Spatial priors (R9). CMB prevalence 17.8 % (60-69 y) to 38.3 % (> 80 y); strictly lobar CMB go with APOE e4, deep and infratentorial with vascular risk [15]; CAA bleeds favour temporal and occipital lobes [16]. A brain-edge rule removes most edge false positives but costs true positives -- distance to the edge belongs into stage 2 as a learned feature.

10. Operating points (R10). For a calibrated score the F1-optimal threshold is half the best attainable F1 [17], so an optimum near 0.3 is expected and the 'hotter' full-data model is a calibration shift. Recipe for a new site: 0 scans -- frozen point plus an alarm when detections per scan leave the development range; 5 scans -- one logit offset shrunk to the development value; 20 scans -- two-parameter calibration, optional conformal control of the miss fraction [18]. Report frozen, site-calibrated and oracle rows.

11. Challenge winners 2022-2026 (R11). No exotic component won: nnU-Net-type networks, Dice + CE, five-fold ensembles, domain-specific preprocessing; ISLES'22 top-3 recipes combined by a 2-of-3 vote beat each single winner [19]; ISLES'24 won on per-modality intensity windowing [20]. Untried here: cross-family vote, the open nnU-Net v2 evaluation, out-of-fold audited post-processing rules, an auxiliary anatomy head, a T2*-specific intensity window.

Nachtrag 2026-10-25: Arena-2 tools built (tested on synthetic data and a template probe; nothing measured on patients yet)

  • Confirmation path: the one arm that 'carries' is trained on the whole pool, its threshold chosen by 5-fold CV over the pool, and applied unchanged to the external public SWI set (never in the pools); one paired test, one-sided 2.5 %, fixed in advance.
  • New stage-2 inputs: the vote count (how many stage-1 runs found the object [14]) and the distance to the brain edge as learned features; venous-atlas values (vein frequency at the candidate, maximum within 3 mm, distance to the nearest frequent vein) from the 3 T SWI atlas of 41 adults [8], carried onto each scan by a contrast-agnostic registration [11].
  • Registration finding (probe without patient data): carrying an atlas with SynthMorph's apply step onto a scan works only if the atlas is first resampled onto the grid of the image that was registered. On a template-derived probe on our 0.5 x 0.5 x 1 mm grid the direct way gave a correlation of -0.02 with the true atlas position (Dice 0.02), the resampled way 0.917 (Dice 0.78); the registration itself was fine (template correlation 0.961). FreeSurfer 8.2.0, mri_synthmorph register -m joint.
  • Synthetic insertion into whole volumes: physics-rendered microbleeds (labelled with their apparent extent) and vein segments at random angles to B0 (no label, signal loss fading over the last 4 mm), placed outside CSF, >= 8 mm from lesions and each other, 70 / 12 / 18 % lobar / deep / infratentorial [15]; no calcifications (same magnitude image as a bleed); a bleed too faint to outline is not written at all; FRST recomputed on the modified volume; the stage-1 map of each copy only from the U-Net of its own fold (four of five fold models had seen the host); only candidates at inserted objects become training-only extras.
  • Realism gate (classifier two-sample test, fixed in advance): pass if real-vs-inserted AUC <= 0.70 for bleeds and for veins and a known-bad fake (smoothed image channel) is caught with AUC >= 0.85; on synthetic data the same generator gave AUC 0.50 and the fake 1.0. Inserted candidates train stage 2 only after a pass.
  • Planned test, user question "should the first U-Net be biased towards sensitivity?": in Arena 1 a blob-loss stage 1 raised the VALDO ceiling from 103 to 110 of 139 lesions but the chain fell (0.594 vs 0.625), and the full union of three seeds lowered it by -0.039 -- both with the old classifier and without the vote count. Arena 2 repeats it the LUNA16 way [14]: union of base and blob-loss candidates (plus the FRST/GMM source where Phase 0 recommends it), vote count as a feature, TrackSet, decision rules v2.

Trägt

IDs re-fetched by script 2026-10-25 with matching titles. Numbers come from the cited abstracts or full texts as read by the agents; simulation results (points 1-2) are the agents' own, from scripts on synthetic data. The v2 rules are implemented and tested on synthetic arms (two real arms carry, eight null arms do not).

Trägt nicht

Most sources were read at abstract level; regulator numbers come from a third-party mirror of the FDA summaries. The label-audit sizes, realism thresholds and threshold recipe are proposals derived from the sources, not validated on our data. No source measures any of this on CMB except points 3, 7 and 9.

Bruchindikator

A first Arena-2 screening run whose case-bootstrap SE differs strongly from the assumed 0.01-0.02; an audit yield near 0 or above 0.5; a realism test that cannot tell even the known-bad fake from real crops.
