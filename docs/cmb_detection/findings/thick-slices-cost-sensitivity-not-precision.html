Thick slices cost sensitivity, not precision -- and hide the smallest microbleeds from image and reference alike

Aussage

VALDO 2021 Task 2 (public), 57 scans in frozen 5-fold cross-validation, split by native slice spacing and by reference CMB size (equivalent-sphere diameter of the 26-connected reference component):

Slice Site Scans CMB found, < 2 mm found, 2-5 mm found, > 5 mm wrong share of 2-5 mm detections
0.8 mm RSS 27 80 38 % (5/13) 66 % (44/67) none annotated 30 %
3 mm ALFA 14 16 none annotated 62 % (8/13) 67 % (2/3) 48 %
4 mm ALFA 8 11 none annotated 45 % (5/11) none annotated 41 %
3 mm, 1 mm gaps (4 mm spacing) SABRE 8 32 none annotated 41 % (11/27) 80 % (4/5) 26 %
  • Within one size class (2-5 mm), sensitivity drops from about 0.64 (contiguous 0.8-3 mm) to about 0.43 at 4 mm slice spacing (ALFA 4 mm slices; SABRE 3 mm slices with 1 mm gaps -- correction 02.11.: SABRE was first listed as 4 mm, the header value is the spacing); within ALFA, same scanner and cohort, 0.62 at 3 mm against 0.45 at 4 mm.
  • The share of wrong detections and false positives per scan (0.4-0.9) show no trend with thickness.
  • On 3-4 mm slices no CMB smaller than 2 mm is annotated at all, and the annotated ones look LARGER (median 12-16 mm3 against 7 mm3 at 0.8 mm), because one dot fills at least one thick slice. The smallest CMB vanish from image and reference, a loss no sensitivity value can show.

Traegt

Reading results of a CMB detector on routine clinical scans (often 3 or 5 mm T2*): expect lower sensitivity at the same CMB size, not more false alarms; report the protocol slice thickness with every result; do not compare CMB volumes across thicknesses.

Traegt nicht

A causal claim: thickness is confounded with site, scanner and rater in VALDO, and cells hold 11-80 CMB. VALDO has no 5 mm scans and only 16 CMB at 3 mm, exactly the clinical range. A controlled test (thicker slices simulated from the same 0.8 mm scans) is planned.

Bruchindikator

The simulation from the same 0.8 mm scans shows no sensitivity drop at 4-5 mm for 2-5 mm CMB, or a rising false-positive share.

Ablesung

Own measurement 2026-11-01 on public VALDO data (numbers may be reported). Data: Sudre et al. 2024, Where is VALDO?, Medical Image Analysis, doi:10.1016/j.media.2023.103029, PMID 37988921. Tier 1b (one dataset, few CMB per cell).
