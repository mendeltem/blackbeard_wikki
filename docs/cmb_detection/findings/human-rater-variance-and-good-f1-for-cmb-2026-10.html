How much do CMB raters disagree, and what is a good lesion-level F1?

Aussage

  • No absolute "good" F1 exists; the human range is the yardstick. Blind, pairwise, from-scratch lesion F1 between two CMB raters has not been published. The best proxy: six raters on 15 subjects (3 T, 2D T2-GRE) scored against an adjudicated reference reach lesion Dice (= F1) 0.52-0.76, median 0.69; definite CMB only 0.58-0.84, median 0.77; per-lesion sensitivity 38-78 %, precision 49-90 % (recomputed from the paper's table) [1]. The two reference experts of that study overlap on 75 of 134 definite lesions, pairwise Dice about 0.72 (derived). The same expert re-segmenting T2 scans reaches 0.82 +- 0.14 [2].
  • Experience and sequence move the human numbers most. Per-lesion sensitivity on 3 T SWI: neuroradiologist 0.905, resident 0.621, neurosurgeon 0.871 [3]; at 7 T four raters found 31-83 % of the definite CMB and count ICC fell to 0.38-0.46 (1.5 T 0.91-0.95), mainly through MISSED small lesions [4]. Six-rater count ICC: SWI 0.951 vs GRE 0.764 [5].
  • Subject level is much better than lesion level. Weighted kappa on 0 / 1 / 2-4 / 5-9 / >= 10 CMB: 0.71, after a joint protocol review 0.82 (747 patients, two readers) [6]. Count ICC and category kappa hide which lesions are marked.
  • Derived human range (3 T T2*/SWI, experienced raters): lesion F1 about 0.65-0.80 (central 0.70-0.75), precision 0.75-0.90; non-expert about 0.52-0.60; same rater twice about 0.82. Treat as +-0.1 (studies of 8-30 subjects, different matching rules).
  • What it means for a detector: F1 >= 0.65-0.70 against one independent rater, or >= 0.75 against a consensus, WITH precision

    = 0.75 lies within rater variability. An F1 above ~0.85 against one rater is above what a rater reproduces on himself and points to a lenient or algorithm-assisted reference, not to a superhuman detector. Our chain (~0.65 against single-rater labels) is in the lower-middle of the human band.

  • Method (round 4, H3): compare the algorithm and the original rater with the SAME new independent rater, per scan: d = metric(algorithm, rater B) - metric(rater A, rater B), with a margin fixed in advance and a scan-clustered bootstrap; with two raters score each separately, not against a merged truth (majority or STAPLE references inflate performance and need many raters [8,9]); 10-20 re-annotated scans support a non-inferiority margin of only about 0.08-0.12, a margin of 0.05 needs about 50 scans (agents' power calculation). Report definite-only and all-lesion F1 separately, and the matching rule.

Trägt nicht

No blind pairwise lesion-level CMB study exists; VALDO released no CMB double reading [7]. Several rows are abstract-level.

Bruchindikator

A blind two-rater CMB study with lesion F1 far outside 0.65-0.80; our own second-rater check (10-20 scans) giving a different band.
