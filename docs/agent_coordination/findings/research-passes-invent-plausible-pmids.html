Research passes invent plausible PMIDs

Aussage

An agent asked to research a topic returns citations that look right and are wrong. On 2026-10-21 three headless research passes (Claude Code, claude -p, read-only tools, network on) were run against the three-data-classes rule. One pass cited Schwarz et al. 2019 NEJM with PMID 31657887 and Rocher et al. 2019 Nature Communications with PMID 31427575, plus a PMC link. Checked against PubMed:

  • PMID 31657887 is "Discrimination, Abuse, Harassment, and Burnout in Surgical Residency Training" (Hu YY, NEJM 2019).
  • PMID 31427575 is "AAV-ie enables safe and efficient gene transfer to inner ear cells" (Tan F, Nat Commun 2019).
  • The correct Schwarz PMID is 31644852, "Identification of Anonymous MRI Research Participants with Face-Recognition Software" (N Engl J Med 2019).
  • The Rocher claim could not be verified at all; the citation was removed rather than repaired.

The numbers the pass reported (92 % embedding recovery, 70 of 84 rank-1 matches) were plausible and the topics were right - only the identifiers were fabricated. A wrong PMID on a real-looking claim is harder to catch than a nonsensical one.

Same trap, one step further in (own error, same day). After correcting the PMID, the figure "70 of 84 rank-1 matches" was copied into the rule as if verified. It is not: PMID 31644852 is a brief report with no abstract, so the number was never read anywhere. An identifier can be perfectly real and the number beside it still invented. The rule now says the figures were not read. A verified id is not a verified sentence.

Trägt

It settles how to consume a research pass: treat every identifier as a claim to be checked, never as a lookup already done. The check is cheap (one EUtils/Europe PMC call per id) and the failure is silent otherwise. It also justifies using several independent passes: the passes agreed on the shape (Turing tiers, DUO vocabulary, output checking) and disagreed on nothing structural, so the consensus carries even where a single citation does not.

Trägt nicht

It does not mean the passes are useless. Two of three produced verified, load-bearing material: the Turing Data Safe Haven tier mapping, the UK Biobank/SAIL/Five Safes output-checking comparison, and the DUO label list. The DUO labels were re-read from duo.csv and matched exactly. The failure was narrow - identifiers - not the research.

It also does not mean a check makes a source true: a valid PMID proves the paper exists and has that title, not that it supports the sentence it is attached to. That second step (read the abstract) stays manual.

Bruchindikator

A wiki entry carries a PMID or DOI that the sources-check tool cannot tie to its title, or that resolves to an unrelated paper. A citation is copied from a research pass without a check, or a "corrected" identifier is written without reading the paper it now points to. The same applies to a DOI that resolves but to the wrong article.

Ablesung
