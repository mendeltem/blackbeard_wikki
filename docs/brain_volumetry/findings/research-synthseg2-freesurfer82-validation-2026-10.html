Literature: how good is SynthSeg 2 in FreeSurfer 8.2 (`mri_synthseg --parc --robust`) for longitudinal volumetry

Aussage

  • Accuracy: SynthSeg reaches mean Dice 0.88 against manual labels on 1 mm MPRAGE (supervised in-domain network 0.91, SAMSEG 0.85) and is designed for raw scans without skull stripping or bias correction [1]. The robust version with cortical parcellation, QC and ICV ("SynthSeg+") is what --parc --robust runs [2]. Thin structures (cortex) lose accuracy first; no per-parcel validation of labels 1001-1035/2001-2035 against manual tracing was found.
  • QC threshold 0.65 is validated for the whole-scan decision: "reject if at least one region scores below 0.65" gave sensitivity 0.999, specificity 0.906, accuracy 0.981, AUC 0.998 against an expert's pass/fail on 594 clinical scans; the value was chosen on a validation set. Per structure the authors applied it (age trajectories) but did not validate it separately [2]. The two reports disagreed here; the full text settles it.
  • Precision: within-scanner smallest detectable change in a six-package comparison ranged from about 1 % (whole brain) upwards, and every package showed systematic between-scanner differences (between-scanner SDC from 4.8 %, SynthSeg thalamus) [3]. A same-session test-retest in an own cohort gave SDC95 0.66 % (brain), 1.38 % (cortex), but 4.67 % for the thalamus, worse than the 2-3 % reported for SynthSeg and SAMSEG in [3] (full-text value, per review).
  • The FreeSurfer longitudinal stream improves test-retest precision by 15-50 % over cross-sectional processing [4]; per-session SynthSeg is cross-sectional, so change measures need recon-all -long or SAMSEG for confirmation.
  • Versions: recon-all 8.0 replaced the front end with SynthSeg, SynthStrip and SynthMorph and gives different output than version 7; 8.1 and 8.2 give the same output as 8.0 (FreeSurfer release notes, documentation, not peer-reviewed) [D1]. Never mix 7.x and 8.x within one longitudinal series. The 8.2 notes also warn that different combinations of build and execution operating system can give different results, and offer fs-allowed-os.txt / fs-allowed-versions.txt to lock them [D1]: workstation and HPC runs of the same series must use the same build and OS, or be compared first.
  • Scanner change: a major upgrade shifted whole-brain volume by 3.4 % in healthy controls; a fixed corrective term was estimated [5].
  • Lesions: in ischaemic stroke, masking infarcts before automated segmentation made hippocampal volumes agree less with manual tracing [6]; evidence on lesion filling is mixed, so keep the unfilled segmentation as a sensitivity analysis.
  • Trust at group level: ICV, brain, GM, WM, ventricles, total cortex, thalamus. Confirm with a second method: hippocampus, amygdala, pallidum, thalamus and all change measures.

Trägt

Two independent reports (35 + 32 references; quellen-pruefen 65 PMIDs correct, 0 wrong, 2 preprint/documentation items without PMID). Threshold numbers read in the full text of [2] (Table 1 and Methods).

Trägt nicht

Most precision figures come from MS or healthy cohorts, not stroke; the thalamus value from [3] was not in its abstract. No peer-reviewed validation of recon-all 8.2 itself was found (only documentation). Pallidum reliability for SynthSeg specifically was not retrievable.

Bruchindikator

A per-structure validation of the 0.65 threshold showing poor agreement with visual ratings, or a validation of FreeSurfer 8.x showing version effects within 8.x.
