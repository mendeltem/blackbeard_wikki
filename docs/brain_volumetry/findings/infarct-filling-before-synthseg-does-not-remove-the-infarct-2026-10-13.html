Filling the infarct in the T1 before SynthSeg does not take it out of the grey-matter count

Aussage

User decision 13.10.2026 evening: "filling before segmentation, implement everywhere". Measured first on the pilot before any rollout: 17 sessions of the 4 reviewer participants, two filling methods, SynthSeg (FreeSurfer 8.2.0, --parc --robust, CPU) on the filled T1, volumes compared with the RAW run: - wm: FSL lesion_filling, WM mask = SynthSeg labels 2/41 minus the infarct, only components touching cerebral WM are filled (the tool refuses others). Cerebellar infarcts are never filled (labels 7/46 are not "WM" for lesion_filling). - mirror: enantiomorphic fill (Nachev 2008): T1 flipped left-right, FLIRT 6 dof onto itself, infarct voxels replaced by the mirrored contralateral intensities scaled to the local level, 1-voxel feathered rim.

Result: the filled region is still labelled as grey matter by SynthSeg, because SynthSeg follows anatomy (shape prior), not intensity. 110 ml territorial infarct (KOP7N936 M0): inside the mask RAW 68.6 ml GM / 36.2 ml WM; after wm fill 63.7 / 43.8; after mirror 57.5 / 43.3. GM total changes by -0.6 % (wm) and -1.1 % (mirror), versus -8.0 % when the infarct is excluded after segmentation. 51 ml cerebellar infarct (L2F7N926 M0): wm changes nothing (no component touches cerebral WM), mirror -1.5 % GM with 39.5 of 46.7 ml still GM inside the mask. 7 ml and 6 ml infarcts: -0.1 to -0.3 %. Sessions without a mask: identical to RAW (the run was a no-op there, 12 of 17 sessions; the --min-ml skip had a bug, fixed afterwards).

Consequence: "fill before" and "exclude after" are not alternatives for infarcts under SynthSeg. Filling changes the image but the count stays; only the mask-based exclusion changes the count. The MS lesion-filling literature (Battaglini 2012, Chard 2010) concerns intensity-driven segmenters (FAST, SPM) and WM lesions; it does not transfer to cortical/cerebellar infarcts under SynthSeg. If filling is wanted for FreeSurfer recon-all surfaces (a different question: surface placement, not counting), that remains a v2 candidate on the HPC pilot. Cost: wm ~90 s, mirror ~220 s per session on the loaded CPU, 4 threads.

Trägt nicht

  • Only 5 sessions carry a mask above 0.5 ml; no test-retest of the filled variant; no recon-all on the filled T1 yet.

Bruchindikator

  • A segmenter whose GM-in-mask drops below ~20 % after filling would make filling a real alternative; SynthSeg does not.
