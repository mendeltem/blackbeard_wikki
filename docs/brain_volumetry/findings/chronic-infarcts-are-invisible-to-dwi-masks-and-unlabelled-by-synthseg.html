Chronic infarcts are invisible to DWI-based masks and get label 0 from SynthSeg; follow-up volumes need a carried-forward mask

Aussage

In a longitudinal stroke cohort the acute infarct mask (DeepISLES on DWI/ADC/FLAIR) exists at baseline but is empty at 6, 36 and 60 months in 12 of 17 reviewed participants, because the chronic scar no longer restricts diffusion. SynthSeg (--robust) then assigns the encephalomalacic cavity label 0 (it drops out of every tissue total AND of the intracranial volume) and labels the gliotic rim as white matter or cortex. The "infarct excluded" variant is therefore complete only at baseline. A new infarct between visits (one participant, 7 ml at an unscheduled 10-month visit) is caught by the DWI mask at that visit only. Rigid alignment of follow-ups to baseline (FLIRT 6 dof, brain-masked T1) reached Dice 0.982-0.990 in all 55 sessions; FLAIR, DWI and ADC registered with flirt -nosearch onto the T1 grid matched in every session; within-participant ICV drift stayed below 1.7 %.

Traegt

Any longitudinal volumetry with acute-phase lesion masks; the decision to build chronic masks (union of earlier masks moved forward + a T1-based chronic-infarct model such as MAPPING/ATLAS) before interpreting follow-up GM/WM/CSF; a "new infarct" flag per session.

Traegt nicht

Cohorts with lesion masks drawn on every visit; small lacunar infarcts that SynthSeg absorbs into CSF labels.

Bruchindikator

A SynthSeg version that labels cavities as CSF; a DWI-based model that detects chronic lesions.

Ablesung

13.10.2026, per-participant review sheets (T1+labels, FLAIR+mask, DWI+mask, counted groups, same slice across sessions); mask volumes per session from the QC page data; label 0 verified by counting labels outside the counting groups (0 ml in all 55 sessions).
