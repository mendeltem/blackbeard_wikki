Aussage

Running every literature question twice, with a different model and no access to the first answer, is cheap and changes the conclusions in a few decisive places. Agreement across runs marks robust advice; disagreement marks places where the literature does not decide and own data or an own experiment must.

Robust (both runs): a scanner change inside a participant is a directed bias, not noise, and must be modelled with a time-varying scanner term [1]; ICV enters as a covariate or residual, the ratio only as a sensitivity analysis [2,3]; the longitudinal stream is more precise than per-session segmentation for change; QC is a cascade of cheap gates plus a human pass on the flagged tail, and outlier exclusion on global volumes increases unexplained variance [4]; mixed models with random intercept and slope, real time in years, no deletion of incomplete participants.

Contradictions between runs (the literature does not settle these): 1. Mirror (enantiomorphic) filling of infarcts. Run 1 calls it the best-validated classical family; run 2 finds no published volumetric validation at all and recommends against it as a default. Classical WM-intensity filling is strongly mask- and method-dependent: with gold-standard masks FSL lesion_filling left 24.5 % of the lesion area classified as GM versus 0.4 % for LEAP [5]; filling fixes misclassification that masking alone does not [6]. 2. Primary stream raw or filled T1. Split across topics and runs; the two ICV/lesion runs and the inpainting run 2 make the raw stream primary and the filled stream a sensitivity analysis. 3. ICV once per participant or per session. Run 1: baseline, time-invariant. Run 2: session-specific; the ICV estimator itself differs between scanners, segmentation-based TIV is more consistent across scanners [2]. 4. Harmonisation. One run favours longitudinal ComBat over a scanner covariate [7], another the time-varying scanner term in the mixed model [1] and doubts that ComBat removes an effect confounded with time. 5. Early interval (0-6 months). Non-linear fast early atrophy (spline terms) versus "mostly measurement error, fit slopes on later visits". 6. WMH tool. LST-AI is validated on MS lesions only [8]; segcsvdWMH reports the best Dice on heterogeneous ageing/SVD cohorts [9].

Trägt

Second-run references (checked: PMID matches title):

[1] Lee H, Nakamura K, Narayanan S, et al. (2019). Estimating and accounting for the effect of MRI scanner changes on longitudinal whole-brain volume change measurements. NeuroImage 184:555-565. PMID: 30253207. Shows: scanner/sequence changes shift whole-brain change measures with a sign; a fixed corrective term per transition in a mixed model improves fit.

[2] Koike S, Maikusa N, Cai L, et al. (2025). Estimation of the intracranial volume is crucial ... Human Brain Mapping. doi:10.1002/hbm.70405. PMID: 41190774. Shows: eTIV and segmentation-based TIV agree only moderately (ICC 0.78) and sbTIV is more consistent across scanners in longitudinal data.

[3] Brzezinski-Rittner A, Moqadam R, Zeighami Y, Dadar M. (2026). Intracranial volume: To adjust or not to adjust? It is not a matter of if, but how. Imaging Neuroscience 4. doi:10.1162/imag.a.1235. PMID: 42206219. Shows: proportional (ratio) correction can invert associations; covariate and residual adjustment agree with a TIV-matched reference.

[4] Monereo-Sanchez J, de Jong JJA, Drenthen GS, et al. (2021). Quality control strategies for brain MRI segmentation and parcellation. NeuroImage. doi:10.1016/j.neuroimage.2021.118174. PMID: 34000406. Shows: Euler number and MRIQC-based exclusion perform best among automated options; excluding outliers on global morphological measures increased relative unexplained variance.

[5] Popescu V, Ran NC, Barkhof F, et al. (2014). Accurate GM atrophy quantification in MS using lesion-filling with co-registered 2D lesion masks. NeuroImage: Clinical. doi:10.1016/j.nicl.2014.01.004. PMID: 24567908. Shows: with gold-standard masks LEAP left 0.4 % of the lesion area as GM, FSL lesion_filling 24.5 %.

[6] Battaglini M, Jenkinson M, De Stefano N. (2012). Evaluating and reducing the impact of white matter lesions on brain volume measurements. Human Brain Mapping. doi:10.1002/hbm.21344. PMID: 21882300. Shows: inserted WM lesions caused GM/WM misclassification that persisted when lesions were masked and was removed by filling with normal-appearing WM intensity.

[7] Beer JC, Tustison NJ, Cook PA, et al. (2020). Longitudinal ComBat: a method for harmonizing longitudinal multi-scanner imaging data. NeuroImage. doi:10.1016/j.neuroimage.2020.117129. PMID: 32640273. Shows: longitudinal ComBat was more powerful for longitudinal change and controlled type I error better than cross-sectional ComBat.

[8] Wiltgen T, McGinnis J, Schlaeger S, et al. (2024). LST-AI: a deep learning ensemble for accurate MS lesion segmentation. NeuroImage: Clinical 42. doi:10.1016/j.nicl.2024.103611. PMID: 38703470. Shows: three-U-Net ensemble beat LST, SAMSEG and nnU-Net on MS data (Dice 0.65 on MSSEG-1).

[9] Gibson E, Ramirez J, Woods LA, et al. (2024). segcsvdWMH: a CNN-based tool for quantifying white matter hyperintensities in heterogeneous patient cohorts. Human Brain Mapping 45. doi:10.1002/hbm.70104. PMID: 39723488. Shows: mean Dice 0.86 across four heterogeneous datasets, +7.8 % over HyperMapp3r and +21.8 % over SAMSEG.

Own data (2026-10-18, cohort-level, relative values only): within the same site, participants who changed scanner show a global scale step of about +1.5 % in every volume including the ICV proxy, which vanishes when each volume is divided by the same-session ICV; this argues for a per-session ICV in this cohort (contradiction 3).

Trägt nicht

  • No run found a volumetric validation of mirror filling; absence in two searches is not proof of absence.
  • The comparison read the short-answer and recommendation sections, not every paragraph.
  • 10 second-run references carry only a DOI and were not checked by PMID.

Bruchindikator

  • A published validation of enantiomorphic filling for structure volumes, or an own simulation (synthetic lesions in a healthy hemisphere, filled vs original volume per structure) that shows a bias below the smallest detectable change.
  • Own data in which raw and filled streams give the same slopes per structure.

Ablesung

For any methods decision: do it where both runs agree; where they disagree, decide by own data (measured step, test- retest SDC) or by a small own experiment, and record the decision with both positions in the analysis plan.
