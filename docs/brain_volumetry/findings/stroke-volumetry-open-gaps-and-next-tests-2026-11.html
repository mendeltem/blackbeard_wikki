Stroke volumetry: what the literature still lacks, and the next tests (Nov 2026)

Aussage

Four questions that block a longitudinal stroke volumetry study have no published answer: (1) a lesion-size limit for mirror (enantiomorphic) filling, or any stroke validation of filling/inpainting for regional volumes [1-4]; (2) the fail rate of the SynthSeg+ cortex QC on routine clinical T1 [5]; (3) the ipsilateral-minus-contralateral thalamus change in %/year [6-8]; (4) a longitudinal validation of any segmentation pipeline in stroke; SynthSeg has no longitudinal stream [9-11]. Own runs must fill these gaps.

Facts that change the reading of own results: - The SynthSeg+ QC regressor reads only the label map, not the image; it predicts Dice for 10 regions and was validated as whole-scan pass/fail, not near the 0.65 cut-off and not on pathology [5]. A low cortex score is a flag, not proof of a wrong cortex. In the own clinical cohort the cortex fail rate is about 24 times that of a healthy MPRAGE reference (DLBS 1.7 %). - Lesion filling can hurt: virtual-brain-grafting fill lowered FastSurfer parcellation accuracy in brain trauma [2]. - Secondary thalamic atrophy is front-loaded (2-4x faster in months 0-3 than 3-12) and the contralesional thalamus shrinks too [7]; disconnection predicts remote change better than lesion volume, only in disconnected thalamic nuclei groups [8, 12]. - Longitudinal processing roughly halves reproducibility error across sites and vendors [9]; Longitudinal ComBat keeps type I error better than a scanner covariate alone [10].

Next tests, ranked: blind visual rating of ~60 visits against the cortex QC (ROC, fails by sequence/site/contrast agent); mirror fill + drop touched structures with a measured size cut-off; disconnection score + thalamic nuclei as secondary endpoints; FreeSurfer -long on 40-60 patients next to SynthSeg, judged by within-person residual SD and slope ICC; change-level QC; SynthSR/FLAIR input only if the rating shows real errors; piecewise time term in the mixed model.

Traegt

Abstract-level evidence for every listed fact; own power calculation: asymmetry SEM 1.8 pp (from SDC95 5 pp), so the main effect needs few complete patients, and subgroups, interactions and informative dropout are the real limits.

Traegt nicht

Full texts were not read; effect sizes per study come from abstracts. The power numbers rest on an assumed between-patient spread.

Bruchindikator

A blind rating that agrees with the cortex QC (high AUC) would make the QC-based exclusion correct and the alternative-input tests unnecessary.

Ablesung

Full notes with all sources: research round folder of the cohort project (00_synthesis.md, 01-04). Own runs: synthetic-lesion test 2026-10-30/11-05, robust size x location test started 2026-11-06.
