Aussage

  1. Per-structure gate (QC score >= 0.65 per structure, as in Billot et al. 2023): subcortical nuclei, white matter, CSF, cerebellum and brainstem were usable in 99.8-100 % of 1253 scored sessions; the cerebral cortex only in 57 %.
  2. The cortex share differs by site from 18 % to 88 %, while the T1 protocol is identical everywhere (Siemens 3T MPRAGE, TR 2.5 s, TI 1.1 s, flip 7 deg, 1 mm isotropic). Scanner model explains part (one model/software version 41 %, another 72 %), but the SAME model at two sites gave 20 % vs 97 % and 30 % vs 57 %. Time point (baseline vs 6-60 months) did not matter (39-47 %).
  3. The QC score is a predicted Dice; on the 24-case example, measured SynthSeg-vs-FreeSurfer grey-matter Dice was 0.80 while predicted scores were lower. A low cortex score means "inspect", not "wrong", until checked against visual verdicts.
  4. Two silent pipeline gaps found by the full-cohort pass: the cohort volume table was empty (rebuild wrote -1 rows) and 237 of 1490 sessions had no QC score.
  5. Growth that atrophy cannot explain (> 5 % between consecutive sessions) appeared in ~4 % of follow-ups, mostly amygdala and caudate: a cheap longitudinal plausibility flag.

Trägt

qc_all.csv and segmentations of the full cohort, T1w JSON sidecars (cohort-level counts only), 2026-10-17.

Trägt nicht

No visual verdicts yet (blind rating page with 53 stratified cases prepared); site effects can also come from patient mix.

Bruchindikator

Visual verdicts that pass most low-score cortex segmentations (then the 0.65 gate is too strict for this cohort).
