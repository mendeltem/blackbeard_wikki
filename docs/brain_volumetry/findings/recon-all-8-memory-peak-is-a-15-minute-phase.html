Aussage

A FreeSurfer 8.2.0 recon-all -all run on a DEMDAS T1w needs its memory only at the start. Measured in scripts/recon-all.log of finished sessions (sub-PDDISDJ1V39UH6_ses-M0 as the profiled case):

phase wall time memory
MotionCor + SynthStrip + SynthSeg + SynthMorph (affine x2, deform) first ~15 min (16:14 to 16:30) SynthSeg VmPeak 43 GB virtual, 26 GB resident; SynthMorph deform VmPeak 20 GB
Nu correction to surfaces, registration, parcellation remaining ~50 min 2 to 4 GB

Whole-run wall time per session (89 finished runs, 24-core box, 62 GB RAM):

parallel runs (MAX_P) threads mean per session throughput outcome
5 4 29 min ~10 per h 174 OOM kills (signal 9), 31 of 61 aborted
3 6 48 min 3.75 per h ran, RAM-bound
2 6 ~36 min (resume run) ~3.4 per h stable, half of the 24 cores idle

Consequence for the full cohort (586 subjects, 1616 sessions, 1652 T1w incl. 40 run-1/run-2 pairs): sequential on the workstation at MAX_P=2 ~20 days; gating only the 15-minute Synth phase (2 at a time) and letting 6 to 8 surface phases run beside it gives ~7.5 sessions per hour, ~9 days; stopping the local LLM server (18 GB RAM) at night allows 3 in the Synth phase, ~6 to 7 days. On a Slurm cluster each session is an independent task: 36 GB, 4 CPUs, 6 h limit is a safe request; 100 concurrent tasks finish the cohort in 12 to 15 h wall time.

Also measured: the 24-case example set finished 61 of 65 sessions (4 without usable input progress); the recon_alle_v2 night run ended 13.10. 00:01 "ENDE: 61/61 fertig".

Trägt

  • recon-all 8.2.0 with the bundled fspython (TensorFlow 2.13 CPU, see linked finding); T1w 1 mm DEMDAS data.
  • Per-run memory gates (MIN_GB) measured on this box; numbers transfer to other hosts as a ratio, not as constants.

Trägt nicht

  • recon-all 7.x: no Synth phase, peak 2 to 3 GB, run-count gating is the right model there.
  • FastSurfer: the segmentation runs on the GPU in about a minute, the surface part is CPU-bound for 60 to 90 min; the thickness is a different method and must be validated against recon-all before mixing.
  • Runs with -no-isrunning resume: a stale recon-all.done makes recon-all skip everything; delete it first (handled in recon_alle_v2.sh).

Bruchindikator

  • A profiled run whose VmPeak after the SynthMorph deform step still exceeds 10 GB.
  • A FreeSurfer release that moves SynthSeg to the GPU or drops it from recon-all (then the phase disappears).

Ablesung

Tier 1 (own logs): derivatives/demdas/freesurfer/alle.log, */scripts/recon-all.log ("#@# mri_synthseg vmpeak", "#@# mri_synthmorph ... VmPeak"), demdas-gm/recon_alle_v2.sh header (OOM count, MAX_P history).
