Aussage

This is the long form of the texts that the DEMDAS GM viewer (QUALITY_CONTROLL/DEMDAS/gm_beispiel_qc/index.html) shows on hover and in its Methods overlay. Written in English on purpose: the page goes to third parties. No patient data here, only methods. Software versions as installed on Alita on 2026-10-12.

Input image. 3D T1-weighted MPRAGE, about 1 mm isotropic, one per session. run-2 (distortion-corrected) is used when present, otherwise the ND image. No bias correction and no skull stripping before segmentation.

Segmentation. FreeSurfer 8.2.0 (freesurfer-linux-ubuntu24_x86_64-8.2.0-20260314-d932c45) mri_synthseg --parc --robust --vol --qc --cpu, every session on its own (no longitudinal coupling). Output: aseg structures plus Desikan-Killiany cortical parcellation on a 1 mm isotropic grid. Volumes = label voxel count x voxel volume, in ml.

RAW / INFARCT EXCLUDED. RAW = every voxel carrying the label, as SynthSeg produced it. INFARCT EXCLUDED = the same after removing every voxel inside the infarct mask.

Infarct. DeepISLES (isles22_ensemble) on DWI + ADC + FLAIR of the same session, computed in DWI space. DWI rigidly registered to the T1 (FSL FLIRT, 6 dof, normalised mutual information); the mask is resampled trilinearly to the SynthSeg grid and thresholded at 0.5. In follow-up sessions the DWI is usually negative, so the chronic infarct is often absent there (a chronic-infarct model on T1/FLAIR is to follow). Infarct side (from M0): infarct voxels inside left- vs right-hemisphere labels; left if left > 2 x right and

0.1 ml, right analogously, both otherwise, none below 0.1 ml in total.

SynthSeg QC. Minimum over the per-structure quality scores predicted by mri_synthseg --qc (0 to 1). Below about 0.65: inspect.

Aggregates (label sets). - Grey matter total = cortex (1000-1035, 2000-2035, plus 3 and 42) + subcortical GM (10-13, 17, 18, 26, 28, 49-54, 58, 60) + cerebellar cortex (8, 47). - White matter = 2, 41 (cerebral) + 7, 46 (cerebellar) + 16 (brain stem). - CSF / ventricles = 4, 43, 5, 44, 14, 15, 24. - Whole brain (GM+WM) = grey matter total + white matter; ventricles not included. - ICV = "total intracranial" from the --vol table (SynthSeg's own estimate), identical in RAW and EXCLUDED. - Brain / ICV (%) = 100 x whole brain / ICV.

WMH (3D U-Net). In-house 3D U-Net (architecture a12), ensemble of the five cross-validation fold models trained on 311 manually labelled cases (CV Dice 0.71), probability threshold 0.5, native space, FLAIR (+T1). Not part of SynthSeg. "-" = not computed for that session.

Cortical thickness. FreeSurfer 8.2.0 recon-all -all -openmp 4, cross-sectional (each session alone), on the same T1. Thickness = distance between white and pial surface at every vertex, averaged per Desikan-Killiany region (ThickAvg in lh/rh.aparc.stats); "Mean thickness" is FreeSurfer's cortex-wide mean. Displayed value = (left + right) / 2, unweighted. "not computed yet" = recon-all has not finished for that session; the page states how many sessions were finished at build time (31 of 63 on 2026-10-12; about 1 h per session, 17-28 GB RAM peak, 30 runs were OOM-killed on 11.10. and are queued again; dicke_nachbau.sh rebuilds the page and copies it to the stick whenever new sessions finish).

Structures (right panel). Volume of every single SynthSeg label, RAW and EXCLUDED; colour = FreeSurferColorLUT.

Image alignment between sessions (display only). Follow-up sessions are rigidly aligned (FLIRT, 6 dof, normalised mutual information, brain-masked T1) to the participant's first segmented session; labels and infarct nearest-neighbour. The panel shows the Dice overlap of the two brain masks (0.982-0.990 for all 40 follow-ups). Volumes are not affected: they come from each session's native segmentation.

Traegt

Reading the DEMDAS GM page and the Excel table DEMDAS_GM_Beispiel.xlsx (sheets Fokus, Volumen_ROH, Volumen_OHNE_INFARKT, QC, WMH_3D_UNet). Any question "which software produced this number".

Traegt nicht

Validity of the numbers (no reference segmentation on DEMDAS); the chronic infarct in follow-ups is missing by construction; thickness hemispheric mean is unweighted.

Bruchindikator

A generator change (gm_beispiel.py, gm_qc_axial.py, recon_alle.sh) that is not mirrored here and in the page's methoden() dictionary; a FreeSurfer or DeepISLES update.
