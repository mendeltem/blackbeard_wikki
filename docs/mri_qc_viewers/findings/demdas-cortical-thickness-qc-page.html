Aussage

Long form of the texts shown on hover and in the Methods overlay of QUALITY_CONTROLL/DEMDAS/cortical_thickness/index.html. English on purpose: the page may go to third parties. No patient data here, only methods. Software versions as installed on Alita on 2026-10-12.

Pipeline. FreeSurfer 8.2.0 recon-all -all, run independently on every session (cross-sectional, one recon-all per session; SUBJECTS_DIR= /home/uchralt/data/derivatives/demdas/freesurfer, log alle.log). There is no longitudinal stream, no group-average template, no -ctk — surfaces and thicknesses of different sessions of one participant are therefore NOT registered to each other. The slope is a simple linear trend of per-session cross-sectional thickness, not a longitudinal measurement (stated explicitly on the page).

Numbers. - Mean thickness lh / rh (mm): ThickAvg of each of the 34 Desikan-Killiany regions in stats/lh.aparc.stats / rh.aparc.stats (distance between the white and pial surface at every vertex, averaged over the vertices of the region), then the unweighted mean over all 34 regions. ThickAvg is what mris_anatomical_stats writes. - Mean thickness (both hemispheres): (lh + rh) / 2, unweighted (region surface areas differ slightly, so this is not the surface-area-weighted cortex-wide mean). - eTIV (ml): EstimatedTotalIntraCranialVol from stats/aseg.stats. - Slope (mm/year): least-squares slope of the line through (months, mean thickness) of all finished sessions of that participant, x 12; shown only with >= 2 sessions. - Plot: one line per hemisphere plus the mean over session months; dotted = least-squares fit of the mean; green band = cohort median of the mean thickness at the M0 sessions (25th-75th percentile band).

Image. ONE axial image per session: T1.mgz in grayscale with aparc+aseg.mgz labels coloured by the FreeSurfer colour LUT (60/40 blend), all axial slices tiled in a square grid (same mosaic style as the GM page). Left/right keys = slice, up/down = session of the same participant, PgUp/PgDn = participant.

Missing sessions. Listed with the reason "recon-all not finished as of "; the page is rebuilt (thickness_table.py + thickness_qc.py) when status_check.sh reports fehlend=0.

Finished definition. A session is finished when recon-all has written both surf/lh.thickness and surf/rh.thickness (same definition as status_check.sh and the acceptance test). If aparc.stats lags the thickness surface by a few minutes, the summary row falls back to the mean of the decoded .thickness surface so the finished set always matches the disk.

Software (versions)

FreeSurfer 8.2.0; Python 3.12 (conda env dl); nibabel; numpy; Pillow; openpyxl (xlsx sheet "Cortical thickness"); Chromium (headless) for the acceptance screenshot. CPU only, GPU untouched.
